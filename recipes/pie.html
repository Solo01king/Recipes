Pie

Description

Pie is a delicious dessert made with a flaky crust and a sweet filling. It's a classic treat that's perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 1 (9 inch) unbaked pie crust
  • 3 cups fresh berries
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
