Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish consisting of a crispy crust topped with sauce, cheese, and various toppings. It's a versatile meal that can be customized to suit any taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 (12 ounce) package pizza dough
  • 1 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup sliced pepperoni
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1 cup sliced bell peppers
